
# Deep learning and alignment of spatially resolved single-cell transcriptomes with Tangram
Biancalani et al., 2021

**Research gap:** Single-cell RNA Seq datasets have cells do not have spatial information but boast a vast majority of genes on the panel. Meanwhile, spatial datasets provide cells with spatial co-ordinates but commonly used platforms allow limited genes on the panel. 

**Solution:** Develop a framework that aligns scRNA-Seq data onto spatial data to map cells back to their original anatomical regions.
